06 Method & Confidence

How the pack is assembled

01Classify every input item: DECISION ITEM, ESCALATION, or NOTING ITEM — when in doubt, escalate.
02Status every metric against its threshold (WITHIN / APPROACHING / BREACH); trend in words only.
03Rate escalations on one severity each; CRITICAL and HIGH end with a stated ask.
04Reconcile the action tracker against the prior pack; overdue first, every overdue row explained.
05Order by severity then materiality — never alphabetical, never chronological.
06Write the executive summary last; lead with the decisions sought.

Sourcing rules applied

R1Every figure traces to a supplied metric, item, or action — nothing invented to fill a row.
R2No fabricated thresholds: the unbounded KRI is flagged as a measurement gap, not assigned an invented limit.
R3No symbols, arrows, or emojis — status and trend are words, so the pack survives any export format.
R4Quiet sections stay quiet: within-threshold, flat metrics carry no commentary by design.
R5The pack reports and recommends; it does not decide. Every ask is explicit.
