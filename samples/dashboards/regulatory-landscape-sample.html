🇺🇸 United States — Federal

Market-structure legislationHIGH
Sustained legislative effort to define which digital assets are securities versus commodities and to allocate oversight between market regulators. Direction of travel, not a finalized statute.
Stablecoin / payment-token regimeCRITICAL
Movement toward a dedicated federal framework for payment stablecoins — reserve, redemption, and issuer-licensing standards. The most advanced US workstream in this illustrative scan.
AML/CFT supervisory attentionHIGH
Continued focus on Bank Secrecy Act obligations for digital-asset intermediaries — registration, the Travel Rule, and transaction-monitoring expectations.
Tokenization & custody guidanceMEDIUM
Growing interpretive attention to tokenized real-world assets and to custody standards for digital-asset intermediaries.

🇪🇺 European Union

Comprehensive markets frameworkCRITICAL
A phased, comprehensive regime for crypto-asset markets moving from rule text into supervised application — authorization, conduct, and disclosure obligations for issuers and service providers.
Transfer-of-funds / Travel RuleHIGH
Originator- and beneficiary-information requirements for crypto-asset transfers, aligning the sector with established wire-transfer transparency standards.
AML single-rulebook & authorityMEDIUM
Harmonization of AML/CFT rules and the stand-up of a central supervisory authority, with digital-asset service providers expressly in scope.
Supervisory technical standardsLOW
Ongoing publication of technical standards and guidance that operationalize the headline framework — background workstream, steady cadence.
