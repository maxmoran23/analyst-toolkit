01 Executive Summary

Meridian Digital Exchange is a fictional mid-size digital-asset exchange used here purely to demonstrate the entity risk assessment output format. The synthetic profile: founded 2019, incorporated in an unnamed offshore jurisdiction, with a reported user base of approximately 4 million and spot and derivatives trading across major digital assets. The illustrative assessment returns a composite risk score of 57/100 — ELEVATED, driven primarily by adverse-media volume, geographic and sector exposure, and an open regulatory matter.

The risk picture in this fictional scenario is concentrated, not pervasive. Sanctions and watchlist exposure screens clean against the synthetic dataset (35/100); financial-health indicators are the second-lowest domain (40/100) but rest on limited disclosure. The dominant drivers are a sustained pattern of adverse media around withdrawal delays, an opaque multi-layer holding structure that obscures ultimate beneficial ownership, and operations centered in a high-risk jurisdiction. No confirmed sanctions hit and no criminal indictment were identified in this synthetic scenario, so the SEVERE override does not apply.

Recommended disposition for this illustrative case: proceed with conditions — escalate for senior review. The matter is workable but not clean; the conditions in Section 06 would need to be satisfied and a senior reviewer would need to sign off before onboarding.

06 Recommended Disposition
⚠

Proceed with conditions — escalate for senior review

In this fictional scenario the counterparty is workable but carries enough concentrated risk that onboarding should not proceed on a desk-level decision. A senior reviewer signs off only after the three conditions below are satisfied and evidenced. If any escalation trigger fires before or after onboarding, the relationship returns to senior review.

📋 Onboarding Conditions

1Ownership transparency. Obtain a complete beneficial-ownership chart down to natural persons holding 10%+, with supporting registry documents for each holding-structure layer. Onboarding does not proceed on a partial chart.
2Independent financial assurance. Obtain the most recent audited financials and a current proof-of-reserves attestation from a named, reputable firm. The auditor turnover noted in Section 04 must be explained in writing.
3Regulatory standing letter. Obtain written confirmation of licensing and registration status in every operating jurisdiction, plus the entity's formal response to the open consumer-protection matter.

🚨 Escalation Triggers

AAny confirmed sanctions nexus involving the entity, a principal, or a beneficial owner — immediate stop and SEVERE re-rate.
BA formal enforcement action, license suspension, or criminal charge in any jurisdiction.
CA material escalation in withdrawal-delay reporting, or any credible insolvency or commingling signal.
DDiscovery that disclosed ownership or financials materially conflict with public record.

07 Information Gaps

The following could not be verified from public sources in this synthetic scenario. Each gap lowers confidence and is the reason the disposition is conditional. Gaps are stated, not filled with inference.

🔍Ultimate beneficial ownership. The multi-layer holding structure terminates in jurisdictions without public registries. Natural-person owners could not be confirmed from public record.
📊Audited financial statements. No audited financials are publicly filed. Revenue, reserves, and solvency rest on the entity's own statements and third-party estimates.
🌐Operating-jurisdiction licensing. Registration status is confirmable in two jurisdictions; status in the primary operating jurisdiction could not be established from public sources.
👤Principal background. Several named executives have limited verifiable professional history before 2019, constraining the governance and integrity read.

Effect on Confidence

Overall confidence is MODERATE. The regulatory, adverse-media, and geographic reads are well-supported by public sources. The ownership and financial reads are not — they rely on partial disclosure and estimates.

Closing the two ownership and financial gaps via Conditions 1 and 2 would likely move confidence toward HIGH and could move the composite either direction depending on what disclosure reveals.
