# Visual Design & Graphics

## Purpose
Create visual assets with an AI assistant — posters, infographics, social graphics,
diagrams, brand materials, generative art. Target professional design quality.

## What you need
Most current AI assistants can produce visual output in several ways: rendering original
designs to `.png` or `.pdf`, generating diagrams from a description, or writing code
(for example p5.js) for generative and algorithmic art. Some setups also connect to a
design tool for AI-assisted layout and editing. Describe the asset — its purpose,
dimensions, content, and any brand colors or fonts — and let the assistant choose the
method available to it.

## Design Types

### Infographic
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Data visualization (charts, icons, numbers)
- Consistent color palette (3-4 colors max)
- White space for readability
- Source citations at the bottom

### Process Diagram
- Flowchart or swim-lane format
- Clear start and end points
- Decision diamonds
- Consistent shapes and colors
- Annotations for complex steps

### Social / Chat Graphics
- Optimized dimensions for the target platform
- Bold typography, minimal text
- High contrast for small displays
- Brand-consistent colors

### Generative Art (p5.js)
- Seeded randomness for reproducibility
- Interactive parameter exploration
- Flow fields, particle systems, fractals
- Export as PNG or animated GIF
