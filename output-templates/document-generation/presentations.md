# Presentation Generation

## Purpose
Produce professional PowerPoint presentations (`.pptx`) with an AI assistant — briefing
decks, pitch decks, training materials, executive summaries, regulatory briefings.
Target consulting-grade design and structure.

## What you need
Most current AI assistants can generate `.pptx` files directly, either through a
slide-authoring tool/skill or by writing the file with a library such as Python's
`python-pptx`. When you ask for a deck, the assistant typically:
1. Creates the presentation
2. Adds slides in sequence
3. Sets titles and body content
4. Inserts images or charts where useful
5. Optionally exports a PDF for sharing
6. Saves the file

Describe the deck you want and let the assistant choose the mechanism available to it.
If you have a brand or theme to apply, say so and provide the colors and fonts.

## Presentation Types

### Executive Briefing (5-8 slides)
```
Slide 1: Title — [Topic], Date, Presenter
Slide 2: Executive Summary — 3 key takeaways
Slide 3: Situation/Context — What's happening
Slide 4: Analysis — Data, findings, evidence
Slide 5: Implications — What it means
Slide 6: Recommendations — Proposed actions
Slide 7: Next Steps — Timeline, owners
Slide 8: Appendix (if needed)
```

### Regulatory Briefing (8-12 slides)
```
Slide 1: Title
Slide 2: Regulatory Landscape Overview
Slide 3: Key Developments (timeline)
Slide 4-7: Deep Dive on Each Development
Slide 8: Impact Assessment Matrix
Slide 9: Required Actions
Slide 10: Implementation Timeline
Slide 11: Resource Requirements
Slide 12: Q&A / Discussion
```

### Training Deck (15-20 slides)
```
Slide 1: Title + Learning Objectives
Slide 2: Agenda
Slide 3-5: Background/Context
Slide 6-12: Core Content (1 concept per slide)
Slide 13-15: Case Studies/Examples
Slide 16: Key Takeaways
Slide 17: Assessment/Quiz
Slide 18: Resources/References
Slide 19: Q&A
```

## Design Principles
- **One idea per slide** — never overcrowd
- **6x6 rule** — max 6 bullets, max 6 words each
- **Visual hierarchy** — title > subtitle > body
- **Consistent color palette** — max 3-4 colors
- **Data visualization** — charts over tables when possible
- **White space** — let content breathe
